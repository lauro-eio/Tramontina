# Tramontina Monthly Leadership Program (9 Modules)

1 company · 2 groups · 9 themes · **12 facilitator hours / month** (6h × 2 groups).  
Plus **3 cierres** (JUL / OCT / FEB) — one after every three workshops.

Folder rules: [`AUTHORING.md`](AUTHORING.md) · [`themes/WORKSHOP-SCHEMA.md`](themes/WORKSHOP-SCHEMA.md).

## Monthly structure

```
                        themes/{code}/   e.g. gty
                                  │
         ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                 ▼
      GROUP A                                           GROUP B
```

You manage short codes. Participants open the **public long slug** (see SHARE-URLS).
