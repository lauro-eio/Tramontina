## Critical Assessment: Operational & Strategic Viability
**Strengths**

    High Grounding & Customization: The scenario successfully incorporates Tramontina’s B2B reality (Walmart fill rates, port demurrage fees, steel imports, credit caps), preventing the exercise from feeling like generic corporate theory.

    Data-Driven Alignment: Using PrinciplesYou aggregate metrics (Logical 74.6 vs. Empathetic 33.9) directly justifies shifting Group A's focus from "soft skills" to "efficiency and risk mitigation."

    Asymmetric Incentive Design: The simulation roles are well-balanced; hidden interests prevent "cheap dates" where one side yields without securing operational trade-offs.

**Risks & Friction Points**

    Tight Simulation Timeline (35 min): Resolving multi-variable B2B trade-offs (e.g., 15k units, Fill Rates, split shipments) within 35 minutes requires intense facilitator pacing; unmanaged groups risk getting stuck in the initial impasse.

    Risk of "Malicious Compliance": High-Demanding and High-Systematic profiles (Group A) may use "risk criteria" as a sophisticated new shield to justify saying "no" under a technical veneer.

    Post-Workshop Escalation Drift: Without explicit, enforcement-backed approval from top directors, participants may default to escalation once real-world pressures resume.

**Key Recommendations**

    Enforce Hard Time Gates: In the simulation, strict time calls at minutes 5, 20, and 30 are essential to force participants into the trade-off phase.

    Director Buy-In: Ensure executive leadership explicitly authorizes managers to trade variables without prior sign-off, legitimizing the new autonomy boundaries.
